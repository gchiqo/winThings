Read Selected lets you hear any text on your screen read aloud in a natural neural voice — without copying, pasting, or switching apps. Select text anywhere (a webpage, a document, an email, a PDF), press Ctrl+Shift+A, and the app reads it to you.

FEATURES

• Works system-wide — select text in any app and press the hotkey.
• Natural-sounding neural voices powered by Piper, running entirely on your PC.
• Comes with three English voices (Ryan, Amy, LibriTTS-R) and a Georgian voice (Natia).
• Georgian auto-detection — switches to the Georgian voice for Georgian text, even mid-paragraph.
• Reading mode — a fullscreen overlay that highlights each word as it is spoken.
• Configurable hotkey — change Ctrl+Shift+A to any combination you like.
• Speed, volume and sentence-pause controls.
• Runs quietly in the system tray.
• Optional start with Windows.
• Fully offline — no internet connection needed.

MORE VOICES

Dozens of free Piper voices in 30+ languages are available. Open Settings → More Voices to listen to samples, download a voice, and add it with the "Open voices folder" button. No restart needed.

PRIVACY

Read Selected does not collect, transmit, or store any user data outside your own device. No telemetry, no accounts. The text you select is processed locally and never leaves your machine.

REQUIREMENTS

Windows 10 version 1809 or later (Windows 11 recommended). Everything needed is included.
